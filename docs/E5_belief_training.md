**Status: not started.** Depends on E4 (its probes are E5's measure). Deterministic dynamics only.

## What it tests

Whether the training history changes the belief of controllability measured by E4's probes:

- immunisation: prior exposure to control before the uncontrollable zone is met;
- similar themes between zones of opposite controllability;
- possibly, the capacity of the latent.

## Measure

E4's probes and measure (b by zone, context from a fixed filtered trajectory), unchanged.
